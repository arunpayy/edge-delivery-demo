Use Case 01 · Document Authoring

High-Velocity Campaign & Product Launches

Traditional CMS workflows often force marketing teams to wait 1–2 engineering sprints for new landing pages. With Edge Delivery, authors compose pages directly in Google Docs, Word, or DA.live using standard headings and block tables, then preview and publish globally in seconds.

  • Authoring surface: Google Docs, SharePoint Word, or DA.live + AEM Sidekick.
  • Key blocks: hero, columns, cards, fragment.
  • Business outcome: Launch promotional pages in minutes while holding 100/100 Core Web Vitals.

Modernization Strategy

Incremental Migration from AEM 6.5 or Legacy CMS

You do not need a risky big-bang replatform to benefit from Edge Delivery Services. Most enterprises adopt a strangler-fig rollout:

Step 1 · Pick High-ROI Paths

Target Top Acquisition & Campaign URLs First

Identify the 10–20% of pages driving 80% of paid acquisition or organic SEO traffic. Route only those URL prefixes to Edge Delivery at the CDN layer (Fastly, Akamai, Cloudflare, or CloudFront) while legacy pages remain untouched.

Step 2 · Automated Import

Convert Existing HTML with AEM Importer

Run aem import with custom mapping scripts to crawl legacy HTML, extract semantic sections and block tables, and generate clean Markdown/Docs content ready for Sidekick preview.

Step 3 · Measure & Expand

Prove Conversion Lift via Real User Monitoring (RUM)

Compare LCP, bounce rate, and conversion between migrated Edge Delivery paths and legacy paths using built-in AEM Operational Telemetry (sampleRUM), then migrate remaining sections iteratively.
